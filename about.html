Our story

A school rooted in Karanja.

Late Rajiv Gandhi Memorial English High School & Junior College has been part of the Karanja (Ghadge) community since 1997.

Our beginnings

Growing with our community.

The school was founded by Late Dr. Ram Chafle in 1997 and is registered under Rashtriya Yuva Uday Sanstha, Nagpur. The school describes itself as the first English-medium school in Karanja tehsil.

Over the years, students have continued into medical and engineering fields. The school’s stated purpose is to support every student’s educational, sporting, social and personal development.

  • English-medium high school
  • Junior college with Science faculty for classes 11 and 12
  • Student participation in sports and science exhibitions
