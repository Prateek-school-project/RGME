A community built around learning
Welcome to our school

A place to learn, grow and aim higher.

Founded by Late Dr. Ram Chafle in 1997 and registered under Rashtriya Yuva Uday Sanstha, Nagpur, the school was the first English-medium school in Karanja tehsil.

Our aim is to support students’ educational, personal, social and sporting development. We are proud of the many students who have gone on to medical and engineering studies.
